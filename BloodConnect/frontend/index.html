ABOUT BLOODCONNECT

Technology connecting people when it matters.

BloodConnect is a Blood Donation & Request Management System designed to simplify the process of finding donors and managing blood requests.

The platform brings donors and receivers together through registration, blood-group matching, location-based donor discovery, request management and donation tracking.
